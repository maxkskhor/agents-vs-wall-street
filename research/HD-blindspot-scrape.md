## Material findings

### 1. Organizational realignment — 30 July 2026
**Not in corpus. Most significant find in this window.**
- Source: [Home Depot press release via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-home-depot-announces-organizational-realignment-to-provide-a-more-seamless-customer-experience-drive-growth-and-capture-market-share-302839484.html), corroborated by [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260730cl16278/the-home-depot-announces-organizational-realignment-to-provide-a-more-seamless-customer-experience-drive-growth-and-capture-market-share), [Modern Distribution Management](https://www.mdm.com/news/top-distributor-sectors/consumer-retail/home-depot-realigns-organization-to-accelerate-pro-growth/), [StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Corporate+News/Home+Depot+announces+leadership+and+organizational+restructuring/26843608.html)
- Merchandising: private label merged into core merchandising (EVP Billy Bastek).
- New "interconnected retail" org unifying customer experience, online, financial services, loyalty (EVP Jordan Broggi).
- Pro operations renamed "Office of Pro Acceleration," now run personally by CFO Richard McPhail, coordinating Home Depot Pro, HD Supply, SRS, Construction Resources.
- New Chief Technology Officer role created (Fran Bell) covering store/supply-chain/Pro tech, AI, data science.
- CEO Ted Decker frames it around 3 priorities: strengthen core, frictionless interconnected experience, "win the Pro."
- TAM now cited as **$1.2 trillion**, up from the $1.1T figure used at the Dec 2025 investor conference (already in corpus) — the increase traces to the HVAC distribution addition (see #2).
- **No numeric guidance attached.** This is a structural/leadership story, not a financial revision.

### 2. SRS Distribution completes acquisition of Mingledorff's — 11 May 2026
**Not in corpus** — and this one predates 21 May, so it's a genuine hole, not just part of the post-AGM blind spot.
- Source: [Home Depot press release](https://ir.homedepot.com/news-releases/2026/05-11-2026-133053552), [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/srs-distribution-completes-acquisition-of-hvac-distributor-mingledorffs-302767796.html)
- HVAC wholesale distributor, 42 locations across 5 southeastern US states, founded 1939.
- Adds HVAC distribution as a new vertical for SRS; this is the deal behind the $1.2T TAM figure above.
- Financially small relative to HD's quarterly revenue base — unlikely to move net sales meaningfully on its own, but is a continuation of the SRS bolt-on M&A pattern already visible in the corpus (GMS acquisition, Sept 2025).
